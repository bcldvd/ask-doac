# App Store metadata (draft)

**Name**: Ask the Diary
**Subtitle**: Podcast answers, cited to the minute
**Category**: Reference (primary), Education (secondary)
**Price**: Free

## Description

Ask anything. Get an answer built only from what guests actually said on The
Diary of a CEO — with every claim cited to the exact minute it was spoken.

Ask the Diary is an unofficial companion for listeners. 228 episode
transcripts ship inside the app, and Apple Intelligence answers on your
iPhone. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to download, nothing sent anywhere.

• 228 episodes, searchable in seconds
• Every sentence cited — tap a citation to watch that exact moment on YouTube
• Works offline. Airplane mode changes nothing
• Private by construction: your questions never leave your iPhone
• History syncs through your own iCloud

Ask the Diary requires Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later).

This app is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed
by, or sponsored by The Diary of a CEO, Steven Bartlett, or Flight Studio.
Transcript excerpts are used to point you to the original episodes.

## Review notes (4.1 / 5.2 risk management)

- Unofficial companion, clearly labeled in description and in-app; no DOAC
  logo or artwork used; icon and name are original.
- Transcripts scraped from public sources; excerpts always attributed and
  deep-linked to the source episode. If review objects, fallback plan:
  contact Flight Studio for permission, or gate transcript packs as
  user-provided content.
- Requires Apple Intelligence: gated gracefully with an explanation screen
  on unsupported devices (reviewers on older hardware see the explainer,
  the app does not crash).
