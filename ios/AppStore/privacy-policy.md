# Ask the Diary — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 31 July 2026_

Ask the Diary is built so that we **cannot** see your data. Not won't — can't.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no accounts, no advertising SDKs, and no
servers of ours to send anything to.

## How your questions are processed

- Your question is understood and searched **on your iPhone**, using a search
  index and podcast transcripts that ship inside the app.
- Answers are generated by **Apple Intelligence**, Apple's on-device model.
  Your questions are never sent to us or to any third-party AI service.
- The app works fully offline. Airplane mode changes nothing.

## Your history

Questions and answers you keep are stored on your device. If you are signed
into iCloud, history syncs through **your private iCloud database**, managed
by Apple and end-to-end associated with your Apple Account. We have no access
to it. Deleting an entry in the app deletes it from your iCloud too.

## Links to YouTube

Citation cards can open episode moments on YouTube. Following such a link is
subject to YouTube's own terms and privacy policy once you leave the app.

## Content

Transcript excerpts of the publicly available The Diary of a CEO podcast are
bundled for search and always attributed to their episode. Ask the Diary is an
independent fan project, not affiliated with The Diary of a CEO, Steven
Bartlett, or Flight Studio.
